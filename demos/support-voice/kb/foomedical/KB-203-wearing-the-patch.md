# Wearing the patch, adhesive and skin care

Wear the patch on clean, dry skin on the upper left chest. Replace the adhesive strip every seven days.

Mild redness under the adhesive is common and usually fades within a day of moving the patch. If you have a rash that spreads, blisters, or itching that does not stop, remove the patch and message your care team.

Support cannot give medical advice. For chest pain, fainting, or trouble breathing, call 911.
