# Booking, rescheduling or canceling an appointment

Open **Appointments** to see upcoming visits. Choose a visit, then **Reschedule** or **Cancel**.

You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before the visit. Inside 24 hours, call the clinic.

Appointment times show in your device's time zone. If a time looks wrong after travel, check your phone's time zone setting.
