# Patch readings are not showing in the app

The patch stores up to 24 hours of blood pressure and ECG readings and uploads them when the Foo Medical app is open nearby.

If readings are missing:

- Open the app and keep it in the foreground for two minutes so it can sync.
- Check that the app has Bluetooth permission and background refresh turned on.
- Check the battery level under **Devices**. Below 10 percent, the patch pauses uploads.

Readings older than 24 hours that never synced cannot be recovered from the patch.
