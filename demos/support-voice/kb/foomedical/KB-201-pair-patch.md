# Pairing your Acme Cardio patch v2 with your phone

1. Turn on Bluetooth on your phone and open the Foo Medical app.
2. Go to **Devices**, then **Add device**, then **Acme Cardio patch v2**.
3. Press and hold the patch button for three seconds until the light blinks blue.
4. Keep the phone within one meter of the patch until the app shows **Paired**.

If pairing fails, turn Bluetooth off and on, then try again. A patch can pair with only one phone at a time. To move it to a new phone, remove it from the old phone first under **Devices**.
