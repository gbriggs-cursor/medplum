# Sending a secure message to your care team

Open **Messages** and choose **New message**. Pick a topic, type your message, and choose **Send**. You can attach a photo or PDF up to 10 MB.

Your care team replies within two business days. You get an email when a reply arrives, but the reply itself stays in the portal.

Messages are not for emergencies. For urgent symptoms, call the clinic or 911.
