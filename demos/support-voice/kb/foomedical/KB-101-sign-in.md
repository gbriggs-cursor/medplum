# Signing in and resetting your password

Open the Foo Medical portal and choose **Sign in**. Use the email address you gave the clinic.

If you forgot your password, choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. We email you a reset link that works for one hour. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within five minutes.

If your account is locked after too many attempts, wait 15 minutes and try again, or ask support to unlock it.
