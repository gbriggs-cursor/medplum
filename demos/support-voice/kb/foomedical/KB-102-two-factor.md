# Two-factor sign-in codes

After your password, the portal asks for a six-digit code from your authenticator app. Codes change every 30 seconds, so enter the newest code.

If codes are always rejected, check that your phone's clock is set automatically. A phone clock that is off by a minute or more makes every code fail.

If you replaced your phone and lost the authenticator app, support can reset two-factor after confirming your identity.
