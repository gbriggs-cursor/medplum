# Send a patient the portal intake form

SYNTHETIC. For front-desk staff at practices that use Foo Medical. Not for patients.

1. Open the appointment and choose **Send intake form**.
2. The patient gets a portal link. Answers show on the chart under **Forms** once submitted.
3. If the link has expired, send it again from the same menu.
