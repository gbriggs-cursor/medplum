# Move a patient's cleaning to another day without losing the reminder

SYNTHETIC. For front-desk staff at practices that use Foo Medical. Not for patients.

1. Open **Schedule**, find the cleaning, and drag it to the new day (for example next Tuesday), or choose **Move appointment**.
2. Keep **Move reminder with appointment** checked. The reminder text is moved to the new day instead of being cancelled.
3. Do not cancel and rebook. Cancelling deletes the reminder, and the new booking only gets one if reminders are on for that chair.
4. The patient gets one text confirming the new day. You do not need to send it yourself.
