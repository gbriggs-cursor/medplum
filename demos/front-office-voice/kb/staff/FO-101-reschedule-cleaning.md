# Hygiene visit on a new day

SYNTHETIC. For front-desk staff at practices that use Foo Medical. Not for patients.

Staff can place a hygiene visit on a different day from the schedule, and the advance text still goes out.

1. Open **Schedule**, find the cleaning, and drag it to the new day, or choose **Move appointment**.
2. Keep **Move reminder with appointment** checked. The reminder text is sent for the new day instead of being cancelled.
3. Do not cancel and rebook. Cancelling deletes the reminder, and the new booking only gets one if reminders are on for that chair.
4. The patient gets one text confirming the new day. You do not need to send it yourself.
5. After the cleaning is on the new day, open the patient's chart and confirm the reminder time moved with it.
