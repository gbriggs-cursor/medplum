# Pull the monthly hygiene recall list

SYNTHETIC. For front-desk staff at practices that use Foo Medical. Not for patients.

1. Open **Recall** and pick the month.
2. The list shows patients whose six-month hygiene recall falls in that month, counted from their latest finished hygiene appointment.
3. Choose **Send recall texts** to text everyone on the list.
4. If a name you expect is missing, report it to Foo Medical support with the appointment date only. Do not send the patient's name or birth date.
