# Reminder texts are not going out

SYNTHETIC. For front-desk staff at practices that use Foo Medical. Not for patients.

1. Open the patient's chart and check **Text reminders** is on and the mobile number is filled in.
2. Open **Settings > Reminders** and check the chair has reminders turned on.
3. Texts go out at 9 AM the day before. A booking made after 9 AM that day does not get a text.
